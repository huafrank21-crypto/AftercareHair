Privacy Policy

AftercareHair
Last updated: May 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AftercareHair (“we”, “us”, or “the App”) handles information when you use our iOS application focused on post‑salon hair care planning, daily check‑ins, and personal progress records. We design the App so your day‑to‑day care content is processed and stored on your device by default.

Summary. Your hair care notes, scores, photos, and optional voice notes you add in AftercareHair are stored locally on your iPhone unless you choose features that inherently rely on Apple’s systems (for example Sign in with Apple, In‑App Purchases, or optional speech‑to‑text). We do not sell your personal care content.

1. Who this applies to

This Policy applies to users of the AftercareHair iOS app. If you do not agree, please discontinue use and remove the App.

2. Information the App may process

Depending on how you use AftercareHair, the App may process the categories below.

2.1 Care content you choose to create (stored locally)

  • Care plan and session details you enter (for example treatment type, dates, text fields).
  • Daily check‑in data such as self‑assessment scores, typed notes, and timestamps.
  • Photos you capture or pick for plan reference or check‑ins (stored as files on your device as part of your records).
  • Optional voice notes you record for personal reminders; audio files are kept on your device unless you use optional transcription (see below).
  • Timeline and history derived from the above to show your progress inside the App.

2.2 Account and purchases (processed by Apple)

  • If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple provides an account identifier and (when shared) limited profile information according to your Apple settings. We use this to associate your local profile and optional coin wallet inside the App.
  • If you buy Care Coins or other consumable items, Apple processes payment and provides transaction metadata required to complete and restore purchases under Apple’s rules.

2.3 Optional speech‑to‑text

If you choose to convert a saved voice note into text, the device may use Apple speech recognition services. In that case, audio may be processed by Apple as described in Apple’s documentation and your device settings. The resulting text is intended for your personal records inside the App.

2.4 Permissions on your device

The App requests access only when needed for a feature you start:

  • Camera — to take check‑in or care photos you choose to save.
  • Photo Library — to select photos you choose to attach.
  • Microphone — only when you record an optional voice note.
  • Speech Recognition — only when you start optional voice‑to‑text.

3. How we use information

  • To provide core App features: planning, check‑ins, timelines, exports you trigger locally, and account/coin features you enable.
  • To maintain integrity of purchases and coin balance where applicable.
  • To comply with law or respond to lawful requests, if ever required.

We do not use your care photos or voice notes for advertising profiling inside AftercareHair’s hair‑care features described in this Policy.

4. Storage, retention, and deletion

  • Local storage: Most information you see in AftercareHair is stored in on‑device app storage (including local database and media folders managed by the App).
  • Deleting the App typically removes local App data from your device subject to iOS behavior and any device backups you control.
  • Delete account (in‑app): Where available, account deletion is designed to remove the local account data associated with that action inside the App, as described on screen. Some Apple‑side records (for example purchase history with Apple) may remain accessible to you via Apple’s systems.

5. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell your personal information. We may rely on service providers that are essential for mobile apps, including:

  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, In‑App Purchase, and on‑device speech recognition when you use it).

Those providers process information under their own policies and your device settings.

6. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate for a consumer app to protect information on your device. No method of storage is 100% secure; protect your device with a passcode and keep iOS updated.

7. Children’s privacy

AftercareHair is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child provided information, contact us and we will advise on deletion steps available on device.

8. International users

If you use the App outside your home country, information may be processed on your device and through Apple services according to Apple’s infrastructure.

9. Medical disclaimer

AftercareHair is a personal care journaling tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. For health concerns, consult a qualified professional.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the new date at the top. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated Policy.

11. Contact

For privacy questions about AftercareHair, contact us through the support channel listed on the App Store product page or the contact method provided inside the App (if any).
